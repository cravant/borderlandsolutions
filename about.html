About Borderlands Solutions

Locally owned and operated junk removal serving East Texas communities.

Serving East Texas

Borderlands Solutions is a locally owned junk removal and light hauling company based in Lindale, Texas. We serve residential and commercial customers throughout the greater Tyler area and surrounding East Texas communities.

Our business was built on the idea that junk removal should be easy, simple, and affordable. We understand that clearing out unwanted items from your property is often a necessary step in moving forward with your plans, whether that's preparing a home for sale, clearing space for renovations, or simply reclaiming usable space.

As a local company, we're invested in the communities we serve. We know the area, we understand local needs, and we're committed to providing reliable service that treats every customer with respect.

Our Approach

We believe in doing the job right. That means showing up on time, working efficiently, treating your property with care, and cleaning up fully when we're done. Our crew is experienced in handling all types of junk removal projects, from single items to complete estate cleanouts.

We provide upfront pricing with free estimates. The quote we give you is the price you pay. None of us here like hidden fees or surprise charges, so we don't do that. Our goal is to make the entire process as smooth and stress free as possible for our customers.

What Sets Us Apart

We're not a franchise or a corporate chain. We're a local business that answers to our neighbors. When you call us, you're supporting a local company and getting service from people who live and work in East Texas.
