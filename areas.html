Service Areas

Providing professional junk removal and hauling services throughout East Texas. Fast, reliable service in your community.

Home Base

Lindale, TX

Borderlands Solutions is based right here in Lindale, Texas. As a locally owned and operated business, we're proud to serve our home community with fast, reliable junk removal services. We know the area, we know the people, and we're here to help keep Lindale clean and clutter-free. Same-day service is often available for Lindale customers.

Major Service Area

Tyler, TX

Located just 25 minutes south of our Lindale headquarters, Tyler is a major service area for Borderlands Solutions. We regularly serve residential and commercial customers throughout the Tyler metro area, including all neighborhoods and surrounding communities. Whether you're in central Tyler or the outskirts, we provide the same professional, efficient service.

Regular Service

Mineola, TX

North of Lindale, Mineola is known for its historic downtown, antique shops, and the Amtrak station connecting East Texas to the nation. We provide regular junk removal services to Mineola homeowners and businesses, including estate cleanouts, furniture removal, and general hauling.

I-20 Corridor

Van, TX

Located west of Lindale along Interstate 20, Van is part of our core service area. We serve residential properties, small businesses, and commercial sites in Van with the same reliable service we bring to all East Texas communities.

Lake Fork Area

Alba & Emory, TX

Northwest of Lindale near Lake Fork, Alba and Emory are growing communities that we're proud to serve. Whether you need cleanup after a lake house renovation, seasonal property maintenance, or estate cleanouts, Borderlands Solutions provides professional service to the Lake Fork region.

Lake Community

Hideaway, TX

The gated residential community of Hideaway, southwest of Lindale, requires professional, respectful service. We work with Hideaway homeowners for all types of junk removal, from furniture and appliances to garage cleanouts and moving assistance. We understand the needs of lakefront property owners.

Tyler Metro

Bullard, TX

Part of the greater Tyler metropolitan area, Bullard is a suburban community we serve regularly. From residential cleanouts to light commercial work, Borderlands Solutions brings professional junk removal services to Bullard homes and businesses.

Tyler Metro

Whitehouse, TX

Another key community in the Tyler metro area, Whitehouse receives full service from our team. We handle everything from single-item pickups to complete property cleanouts for Whitehouse residents and business owners.
