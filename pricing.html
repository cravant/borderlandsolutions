Honest Pricing

Straightforward, volume-based pricing with free estimates and no hidden fees.

How We Price Our Services

At Borderlands Solutions, we believe in honest, transparent pricing. Our rates are based on the volume of junk we remove from your property. You only pay for the space your items take up in our truck.

Before we begin any job, we provide a free, no-obligation estimate. We'll assess the items you need removed, calculate the volume, and give you an upfront price. The price we quote is the price you pay.

  • Volume based pricing (you only pay for what we haul)
  • Free on-site estimates
  • No hidden fees or surprise charges
  • All labor and disposal included
  • Same price whether it's one item or a full load

What's Included

Our pricing includes everything you need for complete junk removal:

  • Professional crew and equipment
  • Loading and hauling of all items
  • Proper disposal and recycling when possible
  • Cleanup of the area
  • Full liability insurance coverage

Factors That Affect Pricing

Several factors determine the final cost of your junk removal project:

  • Volume of items to be removed
  • Type of materials (standard junk, heavy items, hazardous materials)
  • Location and accessibility of items
  • Disposal requirements and recycling options
  • Travel distance to your property

We'll discuss all these factors during your free estimate to ensure accurate pricing.

Special Situations

Some jobs require special handling or disposal methods. We'll always discuss these with you upfront:

  • Hazardous materials may require special disposal fees
  • Extremely heavy items may need additional equipment
  • Items requiring disassembly or special handling
  • Jobs requiring multiple trips or special scheduling

Our goal is to provide complete transparency so you know exactly what to expect.
